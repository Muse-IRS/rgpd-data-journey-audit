# Site Audit Scope

This repository provides a public, user-facing structure for reading a website or digital service through a GDPR and data-journey lens.

It does not produce an automatic legal judgment.

## What can be checked

A public site audit may check whether the site or service exposes:

- legal notice;
- privacy policy;
- cookie / tracker information;
- contact method;
- controller identity;
- declared purposes;
- declared recipients;
- retention information;
- GDPR rights;
- practical way to exercise rights;
- local storage or browser storage signals when observable by the interface.

## What must remain cautious

The audit may identify:

- missing information;
- unclear information;
- apparent contradiction;
- inconsistency between pages;
- difference between declared information and observable behavior.

The audit must not state as final that a site is illegal, unlawful or non-compliant without proper legal review.

## Local action journal

The public interface may keep a local action journal to help the user understand what happened during the audit.

Examples:

- section opened;
- button clicked;
- audit form submitted;
- local result generated;
- export requested;
- local journal cleared.

The journal must be visible and controllable by the user.

By default, it remains in the browser local storage.
